TEACHER DASHBOARD — TAB OVERVIEW
(For PPT — one tab per slide, content kept short)
=================================================

1. STUDENTS
-----------
Purpose: Full roster view of all students assigned to the teacher.
- Filters: search by name, filter by engagement status or section.
- Student Table: shows activity status, last seen, and quiz stats per student.
- Bulk Alert: select multiple students and send a WhatsApp alert in one action.

2. TRACK STATUS
----------------
Purpose: At-a-glance view of student progress across assignments.
- Status Overview: bar chart of students grouped as On Track / Slightly Off / Completely Off.
- Topic Filter: narrow the view to one topic to see per-student performance.
- Student Cards: click a bar to expand cards; tap a card for topic-level breakdown.

3. MOCK EXAMS
--------------
Purpose: Browse all mock exams assigned to the teacher's classes.
- Exam list with title, code, chapters covered, submission count, and average score.
- Click an exam to drill into individual student results, rank, and time taken.
- Date filter (with quick previous/next day navigation) to jump to a specific day's exams.

4. MOCK EXAM ANALYSIS
-----------------------
Purpose: Programme-wide analytics across all mock exams.
- KPI cards: total exams, submissions, and weighted average score.
- Subject breakdown and "students appeared / never appeared" tracking.
- Recent exams deep-dive: score trends, score-band distribution, section-wise patterns.
- Click into any exam for a full summary: score distribution, time-vs-score, flagged
  (rushed / fast-and-perfect) attempts, and a "needs review" watch-list.

5. COMPARE MOCK EXAMS
-----------------------
Purpose: Side-by-side comparison between two mock exams or across time periods.
- Before vs After Summer chart: Class 10 section-wise average scores, April vs June,
  with a Math/Science toggle — shows improvement or drop per section at a glance.
- Pick any class, section, and two exams to compare score distributions directly.
- See student-level score changes and rank shifts between the two selected exams.
